# Product Origination

## Steps
1. **Thesis** through the four lenses; separate structural tailwind from cyclical hype; state what would
   falsify it.
2. **Universe** — seed-word expansion, revenue-exposure purity, liquidity screens; basket weights via
   `thor-calc.optimize_weights` with purity/capacity caps.
3. **Feasibility** — SEC Thailand fit, AUM break-even, fee/cost, distribution channel, competitor map.
4. **Suitability** — target investor, SEC risk level, mis-selling risks; `thor-calc.compliance_precheck`.
5. **Pitch** — IC product proposal; launch is `[IC REQUIRED]`.
