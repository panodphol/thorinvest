# Client Communication

## Steps
1. **Audience** — profile from `references/is/audience_matrix.md` (sophistication, decision role,
   language, channel). Multi-layer clients (RM + end client) get two layers.
2. **Message** — the house view, not a new one. Same numbers, same conviction; simplify words, not facts.
3. **Language** — English, Thai or parallel; Thai keeps institutional register.
4. **Compliance** — `thor-calc.compliance_precheck` before delivery; inject the disclosures it returns.
   FLAG/FAIL → do not deliver to the client channel.
5. **Format** — `kasset-visual-system` for documents; `thor-interactive-presentation` for stage decks.

## Procedures (`references/procedures-index.md`)
Client letter · retail adapter · RM brief · bilingual · panel prep · FAQ · social · video script ·
one-pager · disclosure integrator.

## Knowledge
`references/is/` — audience matrix, format templates, IPS examples.

## Authority
Drafts `[RECOMMEND]`; client sign-off `[CIO DECISION]` + Compliance. Thor never sends.
